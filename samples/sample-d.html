Chapter 02 · Definition

A prompt file is not a harness.

People say "harness" and mean "the system prompt I wrote." That is one of five subsystems — the other four are where most reliability comes from.

→ step through this chapter in four screens

2.1

The five subsystems

Everything outside the model weights, split by what it is responsible for.

01InstructionsRules, conventions, definition of done. AGENTS.md
02ToolsWhat the agent can actually do — shell, tests, linters.
03EnvironmentA runnable, isolated place to work. init.sh
04StateWhat is done, why, and what is next. feature_list.json
05FeedbackEvidence of what actually happened — tests, traces.
NOT A SUBSYSTEMThe promptOne file inside the first tile. Not the harness.

2.2

Why the weakest link wins

A harness is a chain. This is one team's score, and the chain is as strong as its lowest bar.

Instructions3 / 4
Tools2 / 4
Environment1 / 4
State2 / 4
Feedback3 / 4

A brilliant instruction file cannot rescue an agent that cannot run the tests. Adding strength anywhere else is wasted until the weakest layer is fixed.
